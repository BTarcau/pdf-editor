Privacy

Your PDFs never leave your device.

  • All viewing and editing happens locally in your browser. There is no upload and no server that processes files.
  • Files are held in your browser's memory only while the tab is open. Nothing is written to disk except the PDF you choose to download.
  • This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or trackers, and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or images.
  • The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page). These never contain your documents, because your documents are never sent.

The code is open source under the MIT license, so you can verify all of this: github.com/BTarcau/pdf-editor.
